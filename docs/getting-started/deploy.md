# Step 1 – Deploy Yak Ops

Deploy the platform and verify that the required services are available.

## Choose a Deployment Guide

Start with [Docker Compose](/docs/deploy/docker-compose). This existing guide explains prerequisites, configuration, startup, and basic health checks.

## Verify the Platform

Use the checks described in the deployment guide, including `docker compose ps`, to confirm your services have reached their expected running or healthy state.

## Next Step

Once your environment is running, continue with [Step 2 – Connect Your Data](/docs/getting-started/connect-data).

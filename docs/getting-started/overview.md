Read [Before You Begin](/docs/getting-started/before-you-begin) to prepare your environment.

1. [Deploy Yak Ops](/docs/getting-started/deploy) — set up the platform.
2. [Connect Your Data](/docs/getting-started/connect-data) — configure a database connection.
3. [Integrate Your Data](/docs/getting-started/integrate-data) — explore synchronization.
4. [Build Your First Workflow](/docs/getting-started/build-workflow) — learn about orchestration.
5. [Explore Data Governance](/docs/getting-started/data-governance) — discover governance capabilities.
